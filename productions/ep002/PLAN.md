**Outside-the-box choices:**
- **Role reversal as the hook:** the prey stops the predator mid-CHOMP.
- **Ends on a doubt:** the CTA makes the viewer ask "is it a trick?", and EP001 answers it, so the two episodes chain together for binge-watching.
- **For parents:** a bravery theme, "small worm stands up to the bully", which is the book's own message (the author calls it "a comical rhyming adventure about bravery and justice").

## Step 5: Hook and engagement scores

| Measure | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0–3 s) | **8/10** | A CHOMP sound, a giant beak, and a tiny worm saying "Excuse me, Bad Billy…": a surprising role reversal in the first line |
| Curiosity gap | **8/10** | Who is The Big Guy? Will Billy eat Saul? Then the flight and the crash. The final question ("real… or a trick?") leaves it open |
| Humor | **8/10** | Saul's polite courage against Billy's "REALLY?!", "a bigger dinner than fifty of me", "Oh wow! I always wanted to fly!", and slapstick in the crash (Billy clutching his hat; CRASH/THUD) |
| CTA | **8/10** | A cliffhanger question plus "Follow… to find out", with EP001 as the ready answer |
| Overall viral potential | **8/10** | Fast (8 shots in about 43 s), a clear underdog story parents share, a series hook. Honest caveat: virality is never predictable; the weakest link is AI quality on the crash shot |

The first ideas scored lower and were rewritten:
- **Hook (6/10 → 8):** the original opening narration ("But one little worm…") became Saul speaking in the first second, plus the CHOMP.
- **CTA (6/10 → 8):** "Follow for more!" became the "real… or a trick?" question.

**Nothing like the Big Guy problem** (no huge character, no extreme size difference). The riskiest shots are the two fast-motion ones (S05, S07), each held at 6 s or less between drawn frames.

**ElevenLabs credits (estimates):**
- **Dialogue:** about 640 credits (Saul ×4 lines, Billy ×2, narrator ×6).
- **Re-voice:** 40–200 credits for S01, S02 and S06.
- **Crash/thud/chomp SFX:** about $0.36 API-equivalent, unless the owner supplies the sounds (whoosh is synthesized for free).
- **Music:** reused from the cache.
- **New voices:** none. Every speaker (Billy, Saul, Narrator) is already approved, so nothing needs cloning.

**Budget:** $3.14 fits `budget: 4` and `spend_cap: 5`, but **`session_cap: 1` blocks a one-go batch**. Raise it to about 3.5, or generate across sessions.

   - All of these face **right** (as drawn) and are never mirrored.
2. **Code (small, with tests):**
   - (a) a `with: <NAME> <pose>` tag that puts a silent second character into a talking shot (S01);
   - (b) a flying pose on a scene with no ground line (SKY_CLOUDS);
   - (c) make sure `with:` / `riding` never leak into the prompt's acting words.
3. **Kling 3.0 templates** → `library/kling_prompts.yaml` + `docs/kling_prompts.md`:
   - `saul_brave`, `billy_outraged`, `billy_flight`, `saul_riding`, `billy_crash`.
   - Each one keeps the v4 rules: feathered wings, monocle on the LEFT eye, static camera, one speaker.
4. **Owner decisions:**
   - `session_cap`;
