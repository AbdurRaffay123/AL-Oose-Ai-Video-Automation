## Story
The episode answers Jason's video-2 cliffhanger, "What do you think would happen next?". pp.12–13 (the worms playing,
Billy watching) were already animated by Jason, so they appear only as a 2-second establishing move, never re-staged.

The beat:
1. Billy hides high in the oak; the worms play on the grass far below.
2. The Snail, at ground level, looks UP and shouts "WATCH OUT!!!!".
3. The worms panic.
4. Too late: Billy dives from the treetop to the grass.
5. "One, two, three… FOUR!"

## Geography (fixed for the whole episode)
- **Book p.12 layout:** the oak is screen-LEFT, the meadow screen-RIGHT.
- **Heights:**
  - Billy: TREE TOP;
  - the Snail: GROUND, by the oak's roots;
  - the worms: GROUND, out on the grass.
- **Billy's dive:** top-left → bottom-right. His official landing poses face screen-right, so he is never mirrored.
- **Camera height tells the story:**
  - high and wide when we look at Billy;
  - low, at worm and snail eye level, on the ground;
  - one cut from HIGH to LOW sells the drop.
