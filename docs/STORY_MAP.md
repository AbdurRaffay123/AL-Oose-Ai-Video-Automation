# Story map: *Bad Billy the Bird* (complete book)

**Source:** `Al-Oose-content/CONTENT/BBTB MARKETING/Book/BAD BILLY THE BIRD-story.pdf`, 37 pages, all read 2026-10-06
(verse text plus every painting). Narration below is summarised; only key dialogue is quoted (the book is
all-rights-reserved and this repo is public).

**Edition note:** the book interior draws **goggles** Billy (aviator cap and goggles); EP001–EP003 use the **top-hat**
Billy from Jason's official sheets. Big Pea wears an aviator cap and goggles in the book and on his sheet.

## The whole story in one paragraph
Two hungry villains, Bad Billy and Big Pea, fly over the floating sky islands looking for worms for their afternoon
tea. They land on Billy's bragged-about spot and find nothing, so Big Pea storms off hungry, while a worm periscope
quietly pops up between them. Under the grass, four naughty worms sneak out of class, slide down the tree's walkways
and play ball on the grass under the old oak, forgetting **Rule Number One: look up in the tree before having fun.**
Billy is up in that tree, starving. The Snail yells "WATCH OUT!" too late: Billy dives and gobbles all four. Days
later Billy is hungry again and hides in the same tree; when more worms pop out of the stump, he pounces. One small
worm, Young Saul, stands tall and makes a deal: leave us alone and I'll show you The Big Guy, a worm "huge like a
tree". Saul rides on Billy's back across the sky to a steampunk worm island, where Billy crash-lands, reads Saul's
scroll aloud at the rocks, and summons The Big Guy, who swallows him whole. Big Pea, searching for his friend, finds
only the scroll and Billy's shoes. The Big Guy lives on happily with the young worms. THE END, with the motto
"Don't be afraid of the darkness, it's where you learn to shine your light".

|---|---|---|---|

| 2. The naughty worms | 10–15 | Four worms sneak out, play under the oak, forget Rule Number One; Billy dives and eats all four | Playful → sudden danger → dark slapstick |
| 3. The deal | 16–22 | Billy hungry again; pounces at the stump; Young Saul stands tall and offers The Big Guy | Tension → cheeky courage |
| 4. The journey | 23–29 | Saul rides Billy through the sky; crash-landing; the scroll | Wonder → slapstick → setup |

| Epilogue | 34–37 | Big Pea finds a scroll and shoes; The Big Guy at peace; THE END | Wry, warm |

## Page by page
Pages 1–5 are front matter: author bio (Jason Metcalfe / AL-Oose, Te Awamutu NZ), blank, title page (top-hat Billy
flying with Saul on a rocket), copyright, dedication.

**p.6** (no text)
- **What happens:** opening spread, left half. Pastel sky with rainbows and floating islets; an airship with a tiny
  worm crew; Billy's red sneakers flying in from the right.
- **Characters:** Billy (feet only), tiny airship crew.
- **Location:** pastel rainbow sky.
- **Tone:** dreamy, establishing.

**p.7**
- **What happens:** Billy and Big Pea fly in goggles; Billy races ahead, Big Pea lags behind.
- **Characters:** Billy, Big Pea.
- **Location:** rainbow sky, cloud islets.
- **Tone:** zippy, comic.
- **Funny:** the speech bubbles, Billy "SLOW DOWN!" and Big Pea "HURRY UP I'M HUNGRY!!!" (the book's own mismatched bubbles).
- **Key line:** narration: the two hungry villains search the islands for afternoon tea.

**p.8**
- **What happens:** wide shot of a floating tree island with a windmill underneath; a tiny Billy flies in. Inset:
  Big Pea belly-flops on the grass next to Billy, **"DOOF!"**.
- **Characters:** Billy, Big Pea.
- **Location:** floating tree island and its meadow.
- **Tone:** comic.
- **Funny:** Big Pea's DOOF landing.
- **Key line:** narration: Billy led the way; he had bragged about his worm spot since early morning.

**p.9**
- **What happens:** Billy and Big Pea glare at each other on the empty meadow; a brass **periscope POPs!** out of
  the ground between them. Inset: Big Pea blasts off out of frame, leaving a dent in the grass.
- **Characters:** Billy, Big Pea, (hidden) worms.
- **Location:** open island meadow, pink sky.
- **Tone:** grumpy comedy, a secret watcher.
- **Funny:** the periscope; Big Pea rocketing away.
- **Key line:** Big Pea: "I'm out of here, Billy. I've gotta eat something!"

**p.10**
- **What happens:** deep under the grass, a worm school: teacher at the chalkboard, a "DANGER — WATCH OUT" wanted
  poster of Billy and Big Pea, a "Not safe, no play today" sign, the periscope's other end. Four naughty worms
  (blue ball, orange bow, top hat and monocle, aviator cap) sneak out of class.
- **Characters:** the four naughty worms, the worm teacher and class.
- **Location:** underground worm school.
- **Tone:** mischievous.
- **Funny:** the wanted poster; sneaking past the teacher.

**p.11**
- **What happens:** the four worms slide and bounce down rope bridges and ladders under the floating tree to "the
  hole in the wall" ("Danger" sign).
- **Characters:** the four worms.
- **Location:** walkways on the island's underside.
- **Tone:** adventurous, playful.

**p.12**
- **What happens:** the worms reach the sunny grass under the big oak and play ball. The Snail sits by the tree's
  door; acorn creatures with eyes, a spider and a hanging lookout horn watch. The "Look UP in the tree before having
  FUN" sign, flowers and the stump sit on the island edge.
- **Characters:** four worms, the Snail, acorn critters.
- **Location:** the oak meadow (oak, stump, sign, flowers).
- **Tone:** happy, with dramatic irony.
- **Key line:** narration: they had forgotten **Rule Number One: look up in the tree before having fun!**

**p.13**
- **What happens:** up in the branches, Billy grips a brass spy-horn whose eyeball stares down at the worms; a
  **"LUNCH"** thought bubble shows worms on a plate.
- **Characters:** Billy, acorn critters, spider.
- **Location:** oak branches.
- **Tone:** sinister comedy.
- **Funny:** the LUNCH bubble.
- **Key line:** narration: if they'd looked up they'd have spotted Billy's eye; he hadn't eaten in over a week.

**p.14**
- **What happens:** the Snail screams **"WATCH OUT!!!!"**; three worms freeze, the blue ball in the air, numbers
  1-2-3 pop, ghostly winged worms (X eyes) float in the sky as a foreshadowing gag.
- **Characters:** the Snail, three worms, acorn critters.
- **Location:** the oak's base and its door, the grass.
- **Tone:** sudden panic.
- **Dramatic:** the warning comes too late.
- **Key line:** narration: Billy dived to the ground; he could take it no more.

**p.15**
- **What happens:** Billy nose-dives into the flowers by the "Look UP" sign, the fourth worm in front of him,
  number 4 and a ghost worm floating up.
- **Characters:** Billy, the 4th worm.
- **Location:** flowers, sign and stump.
- **Tone:** dark slapstick punchline.
- **Funny:** the count.
- **Key line:** narration: he gobbled those worms, "One, two, three... four!"

**p.16**
- **What happens:** days later, Billy is back on the same oak branch, a lightning-bolt tummy pain, a hot-dog-worm
  thought bubble, a big "CHA-HOO" sound word; acorn critters watch.
- **Characters:** Billy, acorn critters.
- **Location:** oak branches and treehouse.
- **Tone:** comic hunger.
- **Funny:** the hot-dog worm.
- **Key line:** narration: his tummy was empty again, so he hid in the same old oak.

**p.17**

- **Characters:** worms (incl. one in a cap).
- **Location:** the stump meadow, sign and flowers.

## Best unused moments
- **Funniest:** p.15 "One, two, three... four!" (the count is the joke); runners-up p.8 DOOF and p.34 "a scroll and
  some shoes".
- **Most surprising:** p.14 the Snail's "WATCH OUT!!!!", the moment the warning comes too late; runner-up p.9 the
  periscope POP.
- **Most visually interesting:** pp.24–27 sky castle and steampunk crash (owner: flying and crashes look bad), then
  p.11 (worm walkways under the island) and p.12 (the oak meadow).
- **Without The Big Guy:** beats 1–8 and 12.
- **Clear bright outdoor background with grounded characters:** beats 2, 4, 5, 6.
- **Meets ALL criteria:** **pp.12–15, "WATCH OUT!"**: the oak meadow, Billy's first meal, the Snail's warning, the
  count. It is also the direct prequel of EP003 ("a few days went by… EMPTY **again**" refers to this meal), so it
  continues the series' backwards chain.
- **BUT Jason already animated pp.12–13** (worms playing ball, Billy in the tree: `docs/JASON_VIDEOS.md`). Only
  pp.14–15 (WATCH OUT, the dive, the count) are new. **pp.6–9 (Big Pea) have never been animated by anyone.**
